## 2. Decisive architecture: orders are independently actionable; fulfillment is path-dependent

**An order can be commercially released without its counterpart. A physical movement needs verified supply and destination facts, not a fictitious matched order pair.** Do not equate *order issuance* with *logistics readiness*.

| Situation | Independent issuance | Linda may do now | What must wait | Correct object owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. PO confirmed; SO absent and buyer not yet committed** | Issue supplier PO immediately when eligible; no SO requirement | Supplier confirmation, readiness follow-up, obtain availability and pickup facts, preserve thread and document evidence | Buyer delivery booking/dispatch until actual destination and commercial/fulfillment route are known | `purchase.order` plus its Linda request; no synthetic `plasticos.transaction` or `plasticos.load` |
| **B. SO confirmed; PO not yet raised** | Issue buyer SO immediately when eligible; no PO requirement | Buyer communication, destination/appointment constraints, supplier/procurement tracking as applicable | Supplier READY and physical dispatch until purchased supply or verified available inventory is established | `sale.order` plus its transaction if naturally present; don't invent a PO |
| **C. PO for warehouse inventory, no SO intended yet** | Issue PO without SO | PO-scoped vendor communication and warehouse/inbound preparation; use native purchase/receipt `stock.picking` for actual incoming stock evidence when confirmed | Any buyer-facing shipment; load-only commands unsupported by a company-less load | PO, purchase receipt and warehouse; company from PO |
| **D. SO fulfilled from existing inventory, no new PO needed** | Issue SO without new PO | Verify allocated/reserved native stock and use the existing SO-backed load/stock delivery path for outbound execution | Dispatch until actual reservation, carrier, appointment and packet preconditions hold | SO, transaction/load when valid, outgoing picking |
| **E. Back-to-back PO and SO both available, possibly in either order** | Each is issued on its own eligibility | Associate by verified identifiers, allocate movement quantities, proceed through usual coordinated logistics | Movement readiness until its exact supply/destination allocation is proved | Native orders, existing `transaction.purchase_order_ids`, load and pickings |

**Not every missing PO means an inventory sale; not every PO lacking an SO means an inventory purchase.** These distinctions must be based on native order, stock move and destination evidence. Unknown classification affects dependent fulfillment only; it does not create a routine human approval for issuing a valid independent order. For shared/multi-load transactions, evaluate the **actual movement allocation**, not whether every PO linked to an entire transaction has been sent. Never choose the first fuzzy `origin` match.

**Existing Odoo limitation:** at this pinned revision `plasticos_logistics/models/load.py`, `plasticos.load.company_id` is a stored related value from `sale_order_id.company_id`. A PO-only load is therefore company-less. Do **not** create one to represent an inventory purchase and do **not** relax company isolation to allow it. Keep PO-only correspondence on the PO-scoped request, and inventory receipts on native incoming pickings. Any missing inbound scheduling feature is implemented at that existing owner after WP00 establishes its supported seam, or escalated as a **capability gap** before promising automated inbound dispatch. The existing `plasticos.transaction` generally originates during SO confirmation; it is not a prerequisite for PO-first issuance.

## 5. Exact per-order release and email rules

**Creation is not issuance (`BRS-01-R001`, `BRS-01-R065`, `BRS-02-R015`).** Resolve an existing native order first. Where absent, only a verified, source-authorized, complete commercial instruction may produce a native order via the existing authorized Odoo commercial owner; supplier/buyer, company, material, grade/form, quantity, price, terms and applicable onboarding/approval status must come from that authority. No LLM-derived, guessed, first-match or SO-implied PO. Creation and saved draft never send mail, mark a transaction `pending_supplier` or establish warehouse/carrier readiness. If an authorized native creation path is not proven in WP00, mark only that creation capability `UNKNOWN` and block it; do not eliminate the BRS obligation or smuggle new privileges into `user_linda`. A separately legitimate native confirmation/approval then governs that order's issuance.

1. Detect an eligible, live, legitimate **native confirmed-state transition** after Odoo's own approval/commercial checks. PO: transition to confirmed purchase (direct purchase-manager confirmation and two-step approval both covered); SO: transition to confirmed sale. `import_mode`, historical replay, installer, inactive mode, cancellation, reset to draft and unrelated `action_activate` do not create Linda issuance. A native confirmation after a native cancellation is a new release cycle and issues a new revision even with identical terms (SUPERSESSION 2026-10-24). Keep a durable event/request provenance; the tick never backfills arbitrary old confirmed orders.
2. Evaluate the **source order itself**, not a presumed paired transaction: company, supplier or buyer, current permitted contact, applicable vendor onboarding, rightful recipient, native order state, complete material/price/quantity/terms as relevant to that document, party-facing PDF, and applicable pre-existing policy. Only a demonstrably absent authority or material required field blocks that order; counterpart order existence is not a field requirement.
3. Materialize `po_issue` / `so_issue` on the exact source order with required `company_id`; make `load_id` and `transaction_id` optional. The dedupe/touch key is scoped by **company + source order model/ID + purpose + party + material revision + touch**. A PO's lack of SO cannot collide with or attach to a future unrelated SO.
4. Render the native Odoo report and locked audience-specific template. Supplier sees supplier/PO/pickup details only; buyer sees buyer/SO/delivery details only. Verify the PO PDF does not expose a dropship buyer address (G-18). Use Odoo Mail queue with `logistics2@scrapmanagement.com` and native threading (configured, not hard-coded credentials). Do not use `force_send` or auto-followers to leak recipients.
5. On material order change before actual send, invalidate the old request version and **cancel any unsent queued mail**; a new eligible version may issue separately. Check validity before queue processing, not only on request creation. On an already-sent order amendment, retain immutable prior send evidence and route a versioned correction through existing commercial policy; never silently retract or duplicate an external commitment. A real native cancellation of an order whose issue was sent, or attempted with an unknown outcome, queues one `po_cancel` / `so_cancel` notice to that issue's historical recipient; a later reissue to that party never overtakes it (SUPERSESSION 2026-10-24).
6. Reconcile actual `mail.mail` outcome before marking `sent`. `unknown` does **not** mean failed; don't blindly retry when a remote delivery may have succeeded. Record exception only when action is truly needed, otherwise use scheduled reconciliation. If a valid request is replayed or PO and SO are linked later within the same release cycle, create zero duplicate sends.
7. **Do not require a matching order to send its counterpart.** Deferred association does not contaminate recipient selection, order PDF, company isolation or request version.

## 6. Fulfillment / Pending Logistics rule (replaces the two-sends gate)

**Handoff is evaluated for each actual movement, after issuance, not for a transaction's entire collection of orders.** The required evidence is:

- A **known destination** for the physical leg (buyer address, warehouse, or explicitly authorized alternative) and correct company.
- A **known source** for that leg: associated confirmed PO/committed supplier, or verified native stock reservation; if source is pending, continue preparatory messages but do **not** dispatch.
- The **applicable** commercial document send obligations for that leg are reconciled as actually `sent`; no fictitious PO for an inventory sale, no fictitious SO for a warehouse receipt.
- Correct association of any relevant shared order quantities and stock moves; no first result from an ambiguous `origin` lookup, no cross-company links.
- Existing freight, appointment, carrier booking, party confirmation and document prerequisites still apply at the respective later steps; actual **shipper/pickup-site READY** is not inferred from issuance or stock reservation.

**A/PO-first unallocated:** PO request and supplier dialogue proceed; no `pending_supplier` or load required. If later associated with an SO, evaluate that load's handoff anew. **B/SO-first awaiting PO:** SO may be sent while transaction remains `active`; handoff awaits proven supply. **C/warehouse PO:** use native incoming stock picking and PO/warehouse company; no `plasticos.load` and no transaction state. **D/stock SO:** confirmed SO + reserved owned stock is a valid **supply/handoff** route; a separate current, attributable **warehouse/pickup-site shipper READY** from the actual stock custodian is mandatory before carrier packet release or physical dispatch. Supplier PO readiness is not fabricated. **E/matched:** when the actual supply quantities, issue evidence and destination are verified, the SO-backed transaction may move into `pending_supplier` / UI 'Pending Logistics'. Don't count zero matching POs as fulfillment. A load may cover a legitimate partial quantity without waiting on unrelated POs.

If a physical route cannot be represented with existing native purchase/stock/load records, keep the order communication autonomous and **stop only that physical step** as an explicit missing-capability or missing-evidence exception. No force-created placeholder SO/PO, state bypass, zero-company load or invented stock reservation.


### 6a. Physical READY, document identity and guarded sends (bounded BRS interpretations)

- **Warehouse pickup READY (`BRS-02-R004`, `R005`, `R008`):** An outbound SO fulfilled from existing stock must have the actual warehouse/pickup custodian's attributable confirmation that the specified load quantity/material is physically available for pickup at the named site, within an identified available interval, with applicable loading equipment/contact. Store confirmer identity, received/event times, current movement/request/revision and source facility. Native stock ownership/reservation proves supply, **not** physical loading readiness. Retain `supplier_ready` only for the real shipper; do not invent a new vendor or PO for internal stock. If verified warehouse readiness cannot be captured through existing request/response and stock/facility owners, this affected dispatch step waits for evidence, not a new subsystem. Negative/corrected replies revoke dependent physical commitments, not previously sent valid independent commercial orders.
- **PO-only incoming files (`BRS-07-R047`, `R046`, `R049`, `R050`):** An authenticated supplier or warehouse attachment initially binds to **company + exact PO + source request/message**. A document represented as an *actual receipt* also requires the correct **current incoming `stock.picking`**, warehouse and receipt movement/quantity; confirm the native PO↔picking relationship and same company. Multiple partial receipts require matching the specific picking, not selecting one by PO number/filename/email domain. If the incoming picking does not yet exist, retain a PO-scoped unclassified document; never represent it as a verified stock receipt. SO-backed outbound load proofs still require an exact load as defined in the unchanged rule. Link to native Documents and existing attachment/compliance mirror without copying original bytes or trusting hash/OCR as business acceptance. Unknown request/company/picking identity pauses *document classification*, not unrelated valid PO correspondence.
- **What the load guard protects (`BRS-05-R029`):** The canonical `plasticos.load` state/dispatch checks apply to **physical load instructions, carrier packet release and load-state transitions**. They do not govern PO-only/SO-only commercial order **emails before a load exists**; those have *native-order-scoped* state, recipient, company, authority, revision and mail guards. A picking merely marked `assigned` is never proof that a truck was instructed, that dispatch occurred or that pickup/delivery is complete. No independent cron/direct wizard may bypass the real load dispatch guard.
- **Carrier packet proof (`BRS-05-R030`):** The carrier receives exactly **three current and mutually matching Odoo PDFs**, pickup BOL, delivery BOL and DO, with role-safe content. Successful enqueue or Gmail send does **not** equal acknowledgment. Dispatched requires an **attributed carrier acknowledgment of all three documents for that same packet revision**; missing/partial/stale ACK blocks dispatch. No Sales CC on the buyer SO; no archival CC. Odoo audit trail holds the sent originals, not leakage via followers/chatter (`BRS-05-R031`–`R035`).
