## Interpretation that prevents another duplicate system

"SupplyOpportunity" is a representation of current supply. Native `crm.lead` with `type="opportunity"` is the commercial attempt. The material profile describes a reusable stream; the opportunity snapshot describes the material being marketed now. Keeping these meanings distinct does not justify two sales pipelines.

An industrial hypothesis may be useful without being a marketable load. It stays evidence/research until checked. Confirmed buyer demand is a requirement, not the same thing as a facility being able to consume the material.
