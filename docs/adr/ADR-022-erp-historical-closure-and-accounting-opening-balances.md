# ADR-022: Historical ERP Commercial Closure and Odoo Accounting Opening Balances

**Status:** Operator-approved decision; repository admission pending PR review/merge
**Decision date:** 2026-10-29
**Applies to:** Domestic Odoo 19 historical ERP import and source ERP/QuickBooks -> Odoo go-live
**Decision owner:** Scrap Management operator
**Implementation owners:** `plasticos_transaction` (trade/history and document identity), `plasticos_partner_import` (ERP extraction and source mapping), `plasticos_accounting` / native Odoo Accounting (opening GL and open-item realization), `plasticos_commission` (historical payout exclusion)
**Supersedes:** The historical workflow-state *inference* as a cutover status authority in `docs/erp_import_mapping.md` and `native_orders.worksheet_outcome`. Does not supersede ADR-021's SQL -> SourceIndex -> ORM identity architecture or its vendor bill identity ruling.

## Context

Historical ERP records must remain searchable in Odoo without launching duplicate logistics, invoices, payments, taxes, credits, commissions, or stock movements. Historical legacy ERP status codes, ledger postings and receipt membership do not reliably establish whether a balance is still outstanding at go-live. QuickBooks, not the ERP importer, controls the opening general ledger. The source ERP's cutoff aging/open-item reports control the **candidate document-level open amounts**. Their sums must reconcile to the QuickBooks AR/AP Trial Balance at a single exact cutoff.

Odoo invoices and bills have draft/posted/cancelled *posting state*, plus payment/residual state. They do **not** share the commercial `plasticos.transaction` Closed lifecycle. The operator's term 'reopen' means: activate verified remaining open AR/AP and any separately documented operational obligations, not flip every original historical invoice from paid/closed to Draft or Paid.

## Binding decisions

**D1 | Historical commercial baseline.** Every historical `plasticos.transaction` that the importer actually creates starts `closed`, including source status `X` and unrecognized codes. Rows that are excluded or rejected are not created in order to close them. Source `X` still excludes a cancelled inventory inbound from stock and from a commercial deal. The raw source status is provenance only (`legacy_erp_status`) and is not the lifecycle writer. A Plasticos Manager may reopen one selected Closed historical transaction to Active through `action_reopen_historical`, with a nonempty reason and a `mail.thread` audit. Replay must not close that transaction again. Confirming historical sale orders only to compile transactions must remain strictly `import_mode` and suppress procurement, delivery, buyer/supplier emails, accounting posting, AI, and follow-up automations. Historical purchase orders stay unissued/non-actionable; do not fabricate native PO approval, receipt, or `done` status to force a label. No operational reopening from unpaid invoices alone, and Closed does not post, pay, or ship the archival documents.

**D2 | Independent operational continuation.** An authorized source-backed open-operations register may identify specific unfinished transactions, physical loads, purchase/sale commitments, and responsible operators. Only that independently accepted register can transition operational work to active; it must bind source key, company, scope, dates, outstanding quantity and target owner. An AR/AP balance is not such evidence.

**D3 | Preserve original financial evidence without posting.** The existing full historical source customer invoices, vendor bills, freight bills and credits retain their native identities and readable source values as non-posted archival Draft records. Never automatically post, cancel, settle, overwrite their gross lines with residuals, or use them as opening balance entries. An accountant-only audit/recovery route must fail closed for unapproved direct posting, including an ordinary `action_post` on archived originals. Explicit credit/reversal source relationships remain documentary evidence. No guessed payments, reconciliation, taxes, stock or cash.

**D4 | One cutoff and independent inputs.** Establish one signed cutover boundary at the end of the last QuickBooks/legacy ERP accounting day immediately preceding Odoo ownership. Record entity/company, timezone, as-of business date/time, currency, exported report version, immutable raw SHA-256 and preparer/approver for: (a) QB Trial Balance, (b) QB AR and AP aging/control reports, (c) legacy ERP AR/AP open-item statements with credits/refunds and applied-partial detail, and (d) specifically authorized operational exceptions. Freeze/reconcile post-extract deltas up to handoff. If a report is unavailable, stale, ambiguous or mismatched, posting stops. A raw invoice source status never fills a missing report.

**D5 | Opening AR/AP are residual-only Odoo-native items.** Normalize each eligible legacy ERP source invoice, vendor bill, credit note or refund by **source-native identity**, company, party, currency, original reference/date, due date, document type, signed original amount, and *cutoff residual*. An original document with zero residual creates no open item. Partially paid invoices create an opening item for **only the remaining due**, never the full original gross amount and never invented historical payments. Unapplied credits/refunds follow their correct native sign and type. Create and post a **distinct** opening `account.move` of the matching native `out_invoice`, `in_invoice`, `out_refund`, or `in_refund` type, using a dedicated approved AR/AP clearing account on its single opening amount line, zero duplicate historical sales tax, and an unambiguous `CUTOVER` provenance/identity marker. Retain exact original source document reference and a traceable association to the original archival record; do not reuse the original archival `ir.model.data` marker. Historical original and cutoff carryforward are different *roles*, but only one is allowed to contribute the opening AR/AP residual. Opening documents have the source original dates/reference as provenance; accounting entry date and period are validated at cutoff. Use Odoo 19-native semantics for invoice numbering, partner, currency/rate, due date and journal.

**D6 | QB opening Trial Balance + clearing.** Import the signed QB Trial Balance into the Odoo opening journal at the same cutoff, mapping all accounts to the approved Odoo company chart. Replace the **QB AR and AP control-account lines** with *separate AR Clearing and AP Clearing* accounts (do not also post direct opening balances into native AR/AP). Open invoices/bills/credits post the matching native AR/AP lines offset to these same clearing accounts. When the TB entry and opening items are both posted, AR/AP balances equal the QB controls, the open residual schedules equal legacy ERP, and *each clearing account is exactly zero* by entity and currency, absent explicit accountant-approved rounding. No old source revenue/expense is posted a second time. All balance-sheet and current-year P&L accounts present in the QB Trial Balance must be handled according to the accountant-approved chart and tax-period policy; no autonomous retention/closing/equity assumptions. Reconcile opening bank statements separately using native Odoo controls. Physical opening inventory quantity/valuation reconciliation is a separate prerequisite if stock accounting is activated; not an authorization to fabricate stock movements here.

**D7 | Sources must tie before posting.** For every company, account and supported currency: signed QB Trial Balance debits equal credits; legacy ERP open customer invoices minus open customer credits equal QB AR controls; open vendor bills minus vendor refunds equal QB AP controls; QB AR/AP aging corroborates the QB control accounts; every source item has one unique accepted identity, exact partner and source reference. Unknown FX, credit allocation, invoices without a match, stale report revisions, unmatched partners and nonzero clearing residue are **hard exceptions**, never quiet writeoffs or fabricated status. An accountant can document a reconciliation adjustment, but not make the importer silently self-balance.

**D8 | Replay, corrections and recovery.** The source report hash, cutoff ID, source document type/key, entity and currency form the immutable import identity. Same manifest replay creates **zero** further records/postings; a changed report or adjusted residual after final posting requires a separate attributable adjustment/reversal route and new approval, not a rewrite of posted records. Precutover historical draft re-import must not mutate approved cutoff entries. Ledger opening is a single explicit operator-authorized posting event on an isolated go-live database, never automatic as part of `make import-erp` or Cron. Rehearse independent backup/restore and complete rollback from an immutable snapshot before go-live.

**D9 | Commission isolation.** Historical `closed` trades are **not eligible for new commission payout generation** merely because their commission amount is locked or a date cutoff indicates `paid`. Retire inferred historical paid/unpaid from order-date heuristics as settlement truth. The commission engine and payout generator must exclude historical imports by proven source identity in all paths, including manual batch selection, until separately validated commission liability records explicitly authorize a particular payout. Existing commission amounts and source recipients remain documentary evidence.

**D10 | Parallel and handover gates.** During parallel operations, legacy ERP remains the operational source, QuickBooks remains the financial ledger writer, and Odoo runs shadow reconciliation. Production code promotion and financial system-of-record handover are different authorizations. No opening journal posting to live Odoo, legacy ERP deactivation or Odoo payment automation until the accountant and operator approve the cutoff manifest and source-to-Odoo balances. After handover, exactly one accounting writer is active; every late legacy adjustment is separately bridged and traced.

## Required evidence / negative tests

- legacy ERP closed/WORK/NEW/I/C/blank/unknown, and commercial source `X` when a transaction is created, start Closed; raw status stays provenance. Inventory inbound `X` stays excluded. An open AR item **does not** reopen a trade. One Manager reopen with a reason and audit trail can move that one transaction to Active; replay does not undo it.
- Archived full gross invoice/bill/credit stays Draft after compliance and import replay, and cannot be posted by an unapproved UI/server method. Historical SO/PO import emits no pickings, emails, vendor commitments, cash, taxes, or commissions.
- QB AR/AP Trial Balance with matching legacy ERP aging imports **residual-only** native opening invoices/bills/credits/refunds. Zero residual imports none. Opening TB AR/AP lines go to clearing (not native AR/AP), and clearing balances return exactly zero. Partials, credits, refunds, zero, duplicates, partners, currencies and date boundaries tested.
- Mismatched cutoff/totals/partner/company/currency/FX, partial report, nonzero clearing, duplicate source key, unsourced adjustment, canceled source exception and mid-post failure all block posting and leave a recoverable audit trail. Same report replay unchanged; revised report after posting cannot mutate originals.
- Imported closed history cannot generate commission payouts for past periods or marked-paid items, even if the payout wizard is run without an explicit period filter.
- Native Odoo Enterprise end-to-end trial: preflight -> import -> posted clearing zero -> AR/AP aging and QB tie-out -> receipts -> independent restore and replay. Static tests alone are insufficient.

## Ownership and limitations

Retain ADR-021's direct-SQL `reader -> SourceIndex -> ORM` architecture. Normalize *new report evidence* at a clearly owned accounting cutover seam, not a second historic-ERP importer. `plasticos_accounting` owns accounting GL/chart/journals; `plasticos_transaction` owns source-order/document bindings; `plasticos_commission` owns payout selection. Avoid warehouse, Export, web-lead, or unrelated Documents work. The specific cutoff date, actual account codes, source report column map, user/role grants and real report hashes are deliberately **unfilled** until supplied and approved; never guess them.
