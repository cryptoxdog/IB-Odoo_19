**No copied DR architecture, policy, IAM, retention, schedule, code, checklist or claim of independent recovery** in this successor graph. It only adds dependency placement and handoff criteria:

- **Before bulk worksheet attachments or financial posting:** prove a current supported native Odoo.sh Production DB+filestore checkpoint and safe isolated restore. This is immediate change safety, not completion of independent AWS DR.
- **After worksheet originals import/reconciliation:** execute the existing DR contract train with *actual imported* Odoo source, matching filestore, permissions and original image hashes. Preserve Odoo native originals; S3 is independent recovery custody, not a new operational document backend.
- **After accountant's safe opening rehearsal:** prove an independent restore including sample bank/QB/AR/AP/inventory and document-manager exception records. If Odoo Production is subsequently financially posted by explicit approval, re-protect and re-prove **that actual posted state**.
- **Complete original v1.3 contracts** with real operator 1Password emergency pack and clean-room restore. A two-hour RPO is a goal, not demonstrated by four scheduled starts. If provider-supported fresh source is absent, mark relevant contract `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL` and preserve the partial daily recovery milestone; full campaign is NOT DONE.

**Owner scope:** DR owner handles its own provider/access/AWS/Infisical/1Password and restoration contracts. Finance owns financial books, Documents owns original business attachments, Odoo.sh owns platform native backup; this graph creates no duplicate writer or recovery service.

**Exit to Export:** minimum: consistent post-import independent restore has been accepted and any source/permission blockers have a transparent disposition. Full RPO optimization remains separately tracked by the original DR owner. Final release acceptance is not proof of full campaign completion unless all 00-06 receipts exist.
