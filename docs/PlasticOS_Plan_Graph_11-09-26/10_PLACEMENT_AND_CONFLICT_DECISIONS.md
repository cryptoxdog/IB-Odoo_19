# Placement and conflict adjudication record

**Strategic graph placement:** `G-02`, `TAR-01`, `TAR-10`, `C-02` and `C-03` are extant candidates in the inspected v1.19.0 working corpus. Proposed nodes are TARGET/CMT/HYP only; none claims Strategic Authority admission. Plan metadata, audit evidence, BRS rule IDs and capability/activation states are external references, not fifth Plan primitives. No whole-corpus rewrite without the actual most recent predecessor.

**Documents:** existing `plasticos_documents` owns business document verification/evidence; native Enterprise Documents owns original files/versions; `plasticos_documents_native` is integration only. Choosing Export as new verifier is rejected because it duplicates source truth. The duplicate verification flags and native partner-recency matching must be resolved at Documents owner before any AI-assisted automatic association.

**AI:** native Odoo Document AI (when installed) is candidate extraction and classification; CEG/EIE via Gate is the canonical external intelligence owner. Calling Graph engines directly from native AI to accomplish booking parsing is rejected as an unnecessary new transport and semantic-authority confusion. AI can suggest an operation-specific next action, but cannot mint the business event.

**Orders and loads:** the correct conceptual cardinality is an owner-gated allocation across independent native orders and real movements. Deleting current `transaction.load_id` or `load.company_id` relationship now is rejected: its migration and consumer effects are unknown, and user specifically defers operations. Later owner BRS/ADR designs and tests must settle actual representation.

**Outdated source conflicts to adjudicate, not silently change:** admitted v2.2 deterministic seed doctrine vs candidate contextual seed lifecycle; old Graph empty-success fallback vs classified failure; effective group ACL rights vs blanket-delete proposal; Documents native verification surface vs sole-domain-verifier requirement; record-recency match vs exact association. Approval of any business semantic correction belongs to its owner; no automatic code work from this pack.

**Priority comparison:** immediate native Documents proof yields safety and usability for the near-term MVP without additional product scope. Export report/booking groundwork is valuable **only as a parked design**, with its complete 111-rule source and negative cases preserved. Its activation demands separate operator decision and actual native/Logistics proof. Avoid adding irreversible dependencies from future operations onto current MVP.

**Counterfactual:** if the actual Production MVP cannot safely verify and associate existing supplier/buyer documents without first supporting multi-booking or multi-load cardinality, revise the bounded Documents owner gap to the smallest necessary native association and re-evaluate dependency; do not launch the whole Export program by default.
